Questions

Do I need to be fit already to join?

No — most of our members started as beginners. Every class is scaled to your level, and your first class is free so you can try it without pressure.

What should I bring to my first class?

Water, indoor shoes and a towel. We provide mats, gloves for boxing, and lockers with showers.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — up to 4 weeks a year, free, for holidays or injury. Just tell us at the front desk or on WhatsApp.
