How personalisation works

Add these to any template link — the name, phone, address and city change everywhere on the page, including tel: and WhatsApp links:

  • ?name=Heb%20je%20Haar%20weer — business name
  • &phone=%2B31614585841 — phone (use %2B for the + sign)
  • &address=Admiralengracht%2056HS%2C%201057%20GA%20Amsterdam — street address
  • &city=Amsterdam — city

Example: salon.html?name=Studio%20Hair&phone=%2B31653810243&address=Gaaspstraat%2032%2C%201079%20VE%20Amsterdam&city=Amsterdam

Owner bookings page (restaurant): bookings.html?business=The%20Copper%20Pot — shows that business's bookings for the day. Share the link only with the business.

Demo templates only — names, prices, reviews and photos are placeholders to swap for the real business. All five main templates have live booking: taken times, rooms and villas block themselves and bookings save to the business's own bookings page (bookings.html). The five restyled variants are still client-side demos (no payments, no backend).
