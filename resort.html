Good to know

How do I get from the airport?

Faro Airport is 20 minutes away. Add a transfer (€45) when you book, or take a taxi — we will meet you at the gate.

What is the deposit?

One night's deposit at booking, balance due 3 days before arrival. Free cancellation up to 14 days before.

Is the resort suitable for children?

Yes — family villas, kids' club in season, and a shallow beach. Under-12s stay free in a family villa.
