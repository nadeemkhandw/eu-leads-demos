Questions

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome when a table is free, but Friday and Saturday evenings fill fast — booking online guarantees your table.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free up to 2 hours before your booking. After that, please call us — we will always try to rebook you.

Do you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Yes — tell us in the booking notes. We have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes, and the kitchen is happy to adapt most mains.

Is there a children's menu?

Yes — a two-course children's menu for €12, and high chairs are available. Children are welcome until 20:00.
