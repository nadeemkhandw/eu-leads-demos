Questions

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but weekends fill fast — booking online guarantees your stylist and time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free up to 24 hours before. After that we charge 50%; a no-show is charged in full.

Do you do patch tests for colour?

Yes — first-time colour clients get a free patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment.
